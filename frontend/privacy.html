Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This covers altahascreener.in and the Altaha Screener apps for Android and iOS. The short version: you can use everything without an account, an account holds only what you choose to save, nothing is sold or used for advertising, and you can delete it all yourself.

Without an account

Every tool works signed out. A watchlist, a portfolio you type in or import, and the money planner's numbers are kept in your own browser or app, on your device. They are not sent to us to be stored. To work out a portfolio review, the holdings you enter are sent to our server, held in its memory while the review is worked out, and not saved.

Like every website, our server sees the IP address a request comes from. It is used to limit how many requests one visitor can make, and it is not attached to an account.

What an account holds

If you sign in, we keep what you ask us to keep, so it is there on your next device:

  • Your email address. It is how you sign in and where the daily email goes.
  • The portfolio you save: each symbol, its quantity and its average price.
  • Your watchlists and saved screens.
  • Your risk profile answers, if you take the questionnaire.
  • Whether you joined the Altaha Pro waitlist, and which price you were shown.
  • Your conversations with advisers, and, only if you choose to share it, the portfolio you show an adviser.
  • If you keep a wealth desk: the clients, trades and recommendations you enter. If you are somebody's client there, they may have entered your name and email address.
  • A record of which daily emails were sent to you, so that none is sent twice.

There is no password. You sign in with a code sent to your email address, or with Google, in which case Google tells us your email address and that it is verified, and nothing else. A sign-in lasts up to 90 days on a device.

What we use it for

To show you your own portfolio and lists, to work out your portfolio review, and to send the daily email about your holdings. That is all. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or show you advertising.

The daily email

Once you have saved a portfolio, one email is sent after the market closes. Every email has a link that turns it off in one click, with no sign-in.

Usage statistics and crash reports

On the website, we use PostHog to count which pages and tools are used and Sentry to learn about crashes. PostHog may record a visit so that we can see where a page confuses people; everything you type is hidden in those recordings, and so are the portfolio and the planner. These tools see your IP address and browser type. They are not told your email address or your holdings. A browser set to "Do Not Track" is not recorded.

In the Android and iOS apps, neither is used. The apps send no usage statistics and no crash reports.

Who else handles your data

We use other companies to run the service. Each sees only what its job needs:

  • Render runs our server and database, and Vercel serves the website.
  • Cloudflare stores a daily backup of the database. Backups are kept for 30 days.
  • An email delivery provider (Resend or Brevo) delivers sign-in codes and the daily email, and so sees your email address.
  • Anthropic and Groq provide the AI features. A question you type into "Ask AI" is sent to be answered. The explanation of a company's score is written from that company's public figures, not from anything about you.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • PostHog and Sentry, on the website only, as described above.

Some of these companies process data outside India.

Share prices, filings and news come from the stock exchanges and other public sources. Fetching them tells those sources nothing about you.

What the apps can reach on your phone

The apps ask for no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. They use the internet connection, and the phone's own share and print screens when you tap Share, Save or Print.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account, and everything saved under it, yourself and at once on the delete your account page. It is also linked at the foot of every page and from the sign-in page. What is and is not removed is listed there. Backups made before you deleted it are themselves deleted within 30 days.

What is kept only in your browser or app goes when you clear the site's data, or uninstall the app.

Children

Altaha Screener is for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If what we keep or who handles it changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
