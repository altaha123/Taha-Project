Questions

What happens when I join the list?

Your address is saved with the plan you picked, and nothing else happens. Before Pro launches you get one email with the final price and what it includes. Nothing is charged unless you choose to subscribe then.

Does Altaha tell me what to buy or sell?

In the portfolio review, yes: each holding gets a call — hold, trim, average down, add or exit — made by fixed rules from the company's Altaha Score, its industry's trend and the limits you set, with the reasons written out. Altaha is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and the rules do not know your goals, tax position or other savings. Treat each call as a starting point and decide for yourself; for advice fitted to your whole situation, consult a SEBI-registered adviser.

Why would I pay for this?

Because the free site answers questions when you ask them, and Pro keeps answering them when you do not: the email after the close, the story of your own portfolio, the screens you keep coming back to.

Can I leave the list?

Yes — reply to any email from us, or write to us, and your address is removed.
