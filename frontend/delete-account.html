Delete your account

This removes your account and everything saved under it. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and every sign-in on every device.
  • Your saved portfolio, your watchlists and your saved screens.
  • Your risk profile answers and your place on the Altaha Pro waitlist.
  • Your conversations with advisers, both sides of them. If you are an adviser, your profile and the conversations held with you.
  • Your wealth desk, if you keep one: its clients, trades and recommendations.
  • The daily email stops, because there is no longer an address to send it to.

What is not

  • If a portfolio manager keeps a record of you on their own wealth desk, that record is theirs. Ask them to remove it.
  • Backups of the database are kept for 30 days and then deleted. Your data is in them until then, and is not used for anything.

Checking whether you are signed in…

Something not working? Write to hello@altahascreener.in from the address on your account and it will be deleted by hand. See also the privacy policy.
