Money planner

Most planners answer with a savings rate or a grade. This one answers with a date: the month your investments could pay your household’s bills, after which working is a choice. Four numbers produce it, and everything else on the screen shows what moves it.

The arithmetic, in full

The planner works month by month, in today’s rupees, so nobody is shown a frightening figure forty years out and asked to guess what it buys.

  1. Each month, what you have invested grows by the return, is reduced by inflation, and has that month’s saving added to it.
  2. The date is the first month that pot reaches a multiple of one year’s spending.
  3. What is saved steps up once a year, and is smaller while an emergency cushion is being filled and while dated goals are being paid for. It is one pot of monthly saving, split three ways.

It is compound interest and a subtraction, with the working shown on request.

What is in it

Seven tools, one per tab. Each one changes the date or explains it.

Your date
The path to the month work becomes optional, and the moves that bring it closer.
Price tag
What a purchase or a habit costs, counted in days of freedom and not only in rupees.
Goals
Dated goals such as a home or a child’s education, paid from the same pot, each with the delay it costs.
Check-in
One figure a month. It keeps a streak and says whether you are ahead of the plan or behind it.
Budget
The itemised household ledger, and how to split the money on salary day.
Tax
The old income-tax regime against the new one, on your own figures.
Risk
A questionnaire that describes your situation and temperament back to you, with the working shown. It recommends nothing.

Your numbers stay with you

The household figures you type are kept in your own browser so the screen can greet you with your date and not an empty form. They are not sent to Altaha or to anyone else, and “Clear my numbers” removes them. The planner names no product and suggests none.

The risk questionnaire is the one part that talks to the server: your answers are sent there to work out the profile, and are saved to your account only if you are signed in.
